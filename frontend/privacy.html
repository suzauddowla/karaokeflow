Privacy Policy & User Terms

Last updated: September 2026. KaraokeFlow is hosted on alsuza.com.

1. Zero Vocal Data Collection (100% On-Device DSP)

We respect your privacy. When you use the Live Microphone feature or sing along to tracks, all audio signal processing (pitch shifting, reverberation, equalization, and echo) occurs exclusively inside your device's web browser using the Web Audio API. We do not record, store, transmit, or analyze your voice on any server.

2. Microphone Permission

Microphone access is requested strictly for real-time monitoring and applying vocal reverberation through your earphones while singing. You may revoke microphone access at any time via your browser settings.

3. YouTube & Third-Party Content

KaraokeFlow provides an interactive player interface for public educational and musical practice purposes under fair use. Video content is delivered in accordance with standard web protocols. All trademarks, songs, and copyrights belong to their respective creators and copyright holders.

4. Local Storage

Your "Favorites" and "My Songbook" selections are stored locally on your device in your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device.

5. Global Compliance & Contact

This service adheres to global privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA principles) by minimizing data collection to zero personal identifiers. For inquiries or feedback, please reach out via alsuza.com.
